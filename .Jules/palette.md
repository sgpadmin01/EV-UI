## 2025-05-15 - High-Contrast Focus Visible Rings for Dark Cyber HMI Controls

**Learning:** In dark-themed, cyber/industrial touch HMIs with custom elements (toggle switches with hidden `<input type="checkbox">`, custom range sliders, and borderless nav tabs), standard browser focus rings are either completely invisible or hidden by `outline: none`. Additionally, simulating `.focus()` in automated tests does not trigger `:focus-visible`; physical keyboard interactions (e.g. `Tab` and arrow keys) are required. Furthermore, hidden checkbox inputs require styling adjacent visible switch slider spans (`input:focus-visible + .hmi-toggle-slider`) to convey keyboard focus.
**Action:** Use high-contrast theme-aligned outline tokens (`outline: 2px solid var(--accent-primary, #00e5ff)` with subtle cyan glow `box-shadow`) targeting `:focus-visible` across all interactive component classes (`.hmi-btn`, `.hmi-hazard-btn`, `.sbc-btn`, `.nav-tab`, `.hmi-slider`, and `.hmi-toggle input:focus-visible + .hmi-toggle-slider`) without affecting pointer/touch taps. Ensure screen reader labels (`aria-label`, `aria-labelledby`) are mapped to custom controls.
