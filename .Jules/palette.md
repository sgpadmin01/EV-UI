## 2026-08-10 - Enhancing HMI Accessibility with Semantic Tabs and Focus States
**Learning:** Standard HMI pages and custom widgets (like toggle switches and sliders) are often built purely with custom CSS classes, lacking semantic HTML structure. This prevents screen readers and keyboard users from correctly understanding, locating, and navigating controls. Implementing standard ARIA roles (such as role="tablist", role="tab", role="tabpanel") and mapping focus indicators (using input:focus-visible + sibling selectors) creates a fully accessible experience on touchscreen and assistive devices without impacting standard mouse click states.
**Action:** Always map custom interactive controls to standard ARIA equivalents, use aria-labelledby for structural alignment, and ensure hidden inputs (e.g. within custom toggles) correctly delegate their :focus-visible outlines to visible elements.
