## 2026-03-30 - High-Contrast Keyboard Focus Visible Indicators
**Learning:** Pure touchscreen HMIs often lack visual focus indicators when accessed via physical keyboards or hardware controls. Utilizing `:focus-visible` ensures clear visual focus outlines without introducing unwanted focus rings for touch or mouse interactions.
**Action:** Always map `:focus-visible` styles to interactive components (`button`, `input`, custom switches) with glowing high-contrast outlines matched to the HMI design system palette.
