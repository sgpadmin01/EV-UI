## 2026-08-06 - Accessible Tactile and Diagnostics Controls in Embedded HMIs
**Learning:** Embedded touchscreen systems rely on highly visual, custom layouts representing real physical dashboard controls. However, standard range inputs, checkboxes, and buttons lack built-in screen-reader identification. Without explicit `aria-label` attributes, screen readers fail to communicate their purpose to users with visual impairments. Furthermore, destructive or status-clearing actions (like clearing vehicle diagnostic trouble codes) require satisfying, positive confirmation states rather than simply rendering blank spaces.
**Action:** Always map descriptive `aria-label` tags to visual-only tactile toggle switches, slider tracks, and zoom tools. In addition, when diagnostic state data is cleared, replace tables with reassuring, high-contrast, accessible empty states (e.g. green success badges and screen-reader accessible success text) to confirm system health.
